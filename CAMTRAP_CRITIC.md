## 2. C2
Pools: fold-pools ss3-f0 (6 sites, 150 frames), f1 (10, 247), f2 (11, 273) = 27 sites, all PH of one dataset and one season; PH of folds 3,4 and all PL of Serengeti, all PH of CCT/Wel are below the size rule. The dev folds overlap by 75%, thresholds Q are computed per fold-pool and not re-estimated in the bootstrap (stated in 14b item 4), the unit resampled is the site across three pools (not stratified).
Why near-tautological: PH is selected from the label-derived site empty share, the pooled animal prior is then .09-.19 while Q forces the predicted-positive rate to the dev rate (~.4-.5); with AUC .96-1.00, R0 errors are .015-.073, and Q error is about (rate gap) = .25-.32. Q-R0 = +.239 [.144,.349] is the arithmetic of Prop C' on a reader with AUC ~.99; the filter |shift|>=.2 (computed from the same labels) guarantees the condition. What it shows: Q fails when the target prior is far from the dev prior and the reader is almost perfect, in Serengeti S3, which Prop C' already says. What it does not show: anything about natural covariate shift, other datasets (CCT/Wel give no shifted pool), or the size of the effect when AUC is lower (D1). The bootstrap itself is mechanically valid for a 27-site, one-dataset population (site resampling, B=2000); the lower bound .144 is robust to the unadjusted-Holm issue, but the population is 3 folds of one set, so the inference target is "these Serengeti PH sites".

## 3. C3
- Certificate computed correctly (1.1). r for Q equals about 1-rho by construction, so rho(Q) is a per-fold constant independent of the pool (CCT/Wel .06-.23/.09-.12, Serengeti .24-.27); only R0's rho uses target information (its predicted-negative rate).
- 28 pools, 56 cases, dependent: R0 and Q share a pool; CCT PL is 85% of P0 sites; folds share 75% of dev sites. Two misses, both Q in Serengeti PH: f0 regret .253 > rho .244 (shift -.28, INSIDE the +-.30 band, so a genuine miss under the model: estimation error of the source CDFs, small 150-frame pool, or class-conditional shift, theory-84 H2 says these are not propagated) and f2 .308 > .264 (shift -.32, outside the band, expected). 54/56 = .964: one more miss gives .946 and fails the 95% bar; exact binomial lower 95% bound for 54/56 is about .88. The pass is by margin of one case and the cases are not independent.
- Stratify: unshifted pools (|shift|<.2): 50/50 covered. Shifted pools: R0 3/3 (rho .45-.54 vs regret .004-.033, 15-100x slack), Q 1/3. The certificate is a statement made for the case the data barely contain.
- Vacuity: median realised regret in non-PH cases is .010 (n=50), median rho .115; median rho/regret = 14x (p10 2.1x); rho is comparable to the total error (.05-.20) in CCT/Wel where the oracle is not better than the majority rule. The +-.30 band is 3x wider than the realised shifts (<=.10 in CCT/Wel). The 'informative' bar (mean width <= .30) equals the band half-width and is set in the plan without relation to regret or to a decision (e.g. choosing Q vs R0 needs rho below the observed difference ~.01-.03: not met in any case). It is a weak bar: passing it says nothing about usefulness. Mean width .177 is also an average over R0 and Q across pools of different composition.
- Correct use per theory-84: Reg uses source CDFs from the 25% calibration sites (40 quantile bins, +.5 smoothing), band centred at the calibration prior clipped to [.02,.98]; coverage is guaranteed only inside the common-monotone-map model with exact CDFs and the band containing the truth, and is label-testable only through coverage as done here. The sample-level regret vs in-sample oracle is conservative for coverage (oracle optimism), so the 54/56 is not inflated by that.

## 4. C4
- Design: audit pool = union of audit sites over folds (CCT 7x5=35 sites, Wellington 10x5=50, Serengeti 10x5=50, from 110/150/150); k in {5,10,20} drawn without replacement 2000 times from ONE fixed audit pool (ct_run.py:163-170); truth = pooled R0 error on the eval frames of all folds with fold-specific R0, consistent with the audited rule (ct_run.py:159, 160). Consistent definition: yes.
- "Cover 1.0" is the share of subsamples of one realised pool whose bound exceeds one realised truth; it is a function of that single pool's mean error relative to truth. It is not coverage over independent audit-pool draws (that would need repeated site splits/seeds). With U far above truth (betting mean U .24-.64 vs truth .055-.181: ratios CCT 4.6/3.4/2.5x at k=5/10/20, Wel 3.5/2.5/1.9x, Serengeti 10.6/6.9/4.4x), 1.0 is nearly forced; for k=20 of 35 sites the 2000 draws overlap heavily. Validity of the bound (Theorem 1 of theory/82) is not tested by this and not contradicted either.
- CP cover .71-.97 (7 of 9 cells below .95): consistent in direction with undercoverage under within-site correlation, but the same single-pool artefact applies, so no reproducibility or size can be claimed. The truth is also a finite-population error of the evaluated sites, not the population mean the bound targets.

## Summary
