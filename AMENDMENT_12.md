# Amendment 12 (append-only) — real-robot gripper-state study (LeRobot), registered before any reader output, 2026-10-28
Made BEFORE any feature, logit or probe output exists for these data (no feat_*, fit_* or result file in experiments/out/lerobot). PREREGISTRATION.md is unchanged (SHA-256 886896e6c49f2ec4d369d6ae9fd132be1018027042f61e9f9129eabeba545fd2). The results of SIM4 and SIM5 (Amendments 5-11) are known to the authors and informed this design: the room-for-improvement condition below is the lesson of the SIM5 floor effect.
Data: real robot wrist-camera video; label = measured gripper joint value (observation.state), a sensor reading, not a human or AI label. Datasets used: lerobot/utaustin_mutex (150 of 1500 episodes), lerobot/austin_sirius_dataset (150 of 559), lerobot/droid_100 (100). All were used before in unrelated analyses (H84-H96: action/language work, not state reading with threshold repair); they are not new data and not independent of the authors' earlier work. lerobot/stanford_hydra_dataset is EXCLUDED from all analysis because a visual check (24 frames) found the fingers mostly invisible (14 of 24 frames show no finger or held object; 8 disagreements with the label); lerobot/cmu_play_fusion was dropped earlier for the same reason (20 of 24 not decidable). Both exclusions are based on the visual check only, made before any reader output. Label rule (values only, written before any frame was decoded; band cut-offs .15/.40 chosen after seeing the value histogram, not changed since): open if u <= .15, closed if u >= .40, between excluded.
Known limits: the time-only baseline (cross-validated by episode) has error .297 (droid), .146 (mutex), .278 (sirius), all < .35, so the label is partly predictable from phase; mutex 'open' is recognised partly by the absence of finger pads (10 of 24 viewed frames); montage agreement was 18/24 droid, 16/24 mutex, 18/24 sirius with the rest disagreeing or undecidable, viewed by one builder agent only; image resolutions differ (84-128 px upsampled).
Design (experiments/t2_lr.py, unit = episode): ordered cross-dataset pairs among mutex, sirius, droid (6) plus within-dataset cells (3, no-shift reference); the source dataset provides probe training (2/3 of episodes) and calibration (1/3); the target dataset provides audit (1/3) and evaluation (2/3) episodes; 30 corruption cells on target frames. Frames are class-balanced per episode BEFORE any probe (cap the majority class per episode at the minority count), which makes the open share exactly .5 in every dataset: therefore NO prior shift exists in this study and Proposition C' is not tested (the condition of Theorem A holds by construction). Every cell reports a time-only baseline (threshold on t, on t/T, or depth-3 tree) and a random-label placebo probe; a cell is 'informative' only if the R0 error in the clean cell is below the time-only error minus .05; claims are restricted to informative cross cells.
Counting rules: C1 counts a pair when Q beats R0 by >= .01 in the clean cell, the cell is informative and err(R0)-err(oracle) >= .01 (room exists); cells without room are reported and excluded; the claim needs >= 3 such pairs, otherwise the answer is 'not testable'. C2, C3, C4 as in Amendment 10 on informative cross cells, with the audit pool required to be >= 2m frames; C3 measures the cost of halving the label sample (Amendment 11) and is reported as such; its per-dataset intervals still use audit-episode resampling with replacement (the defect of Amendment 11 item 4 is not fixed). Holm over the four p-values.
Not met by design: Tier 1 (3 datasets, same labs reused, 100-150 episodes, one-observer visual check, no prior shift). Whatever the outcome it cannot support a confirmatory claim.
