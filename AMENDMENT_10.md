Amendments 4-9 concern SIM4 and E5; their results are known to the authors and informed this design (in particular C2/C4 failed and C1 held in SIM4), so SIM5 is a replication on new families by the same authors, not an independent test.
Datasets (MuJoCo, label = exact joint state read after mj_forward, guard band excluded and never rendered): microwave (vertical-hinge door), cabinet2 (two doors), boxlid, laptop (screen hinge), slider (sliding-door cabinet). Each: 300 scenes (150 dev-domain, 150 eval-domain), 4 frames per scene (2 closed, 2 open), 1200 frames. Domains differ on four axes (light, texture/colour, camera, distractors) with disjoint dev/eval levels; each eval scene shifts 2-4 axes. The shift is designed by the authors and is not natural. Generator checks: open share .5 per scene, 0 frames with the moving part under 150 px; the builder viewed 120 frames of the final montages and all labels matched.
Analysis: PREREGISTRATION sections 3-5 via experiments/t2_s5.py (imports t2_s4.py, t2_run.py, t2_b2.py unchanged). Group = scene. Dev-domain scenes 100 train / 50 calibration; eval-domain scenes 50 audit (200 frames) / 100 evaluation; 30 corruption cells on eval-domain frames only; no time axis, so no phase baseline (a random-label placebo probe is the chance reference). Counting rules fixed here: C1 counts a dataset when Q beats R0 by >= .01 in the clean eval-domain cell, claim needs >= 3 of 5 datasets (third-largest scene-bootstrap replicate); C3 counts a dataset when BA-full beats LA by >= .02 at m = 100 with bootstrap lower bound > 0, needs >= 3 of 5; C2 and C4 as registered; Holm over the four p-values. Per-dataset C3 intervals use the same number of audit draws (40) as the point estimate (defect of Amendment 5 fixed).
Limits stated in advance: simulation only; one engine (MuJoCo); five author-built families; the shift magnitude was not calibrated against the reader; the darkest eval scenes may collapse the reader; a confirmatory claim is about these simulated datasets only. Not natural data; it does not satisfy the registered Tier 1 requirement of natural shift.
