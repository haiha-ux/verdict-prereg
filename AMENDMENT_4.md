# Amendment 4 (append-only) — simulated confirmatory study (SIM4), 2026-10-27
Made BEFORE any feature, logit or probe output exists for SIM4 (no feat_*, fit_* or result file exists in experiments/out/sim4 at this moment). The registered file PREREGISTRATION.md is unchanged (SHA-256 886896e6c49f2ec4d369d6ae9fd132be1018027042f61e9f9129eabeba545fd2). Amendments 1-3 concern BEHAVIOR-1K task-0004, which the authors consider unusable as evidence (Amendment 3).
Why SIM4: no real dataset with ground truth not produced by an AI, enough independent groups, a scene split and a label not predictable from time could be obtained (RBO too small, DAVIAN single scene, BEHAVIOR-1K task-0004 phase-confounded and tasks 0002/0004 often not visible to the head camera, EPIC-STATES not downloadable). SIM4 is the authors' own generator.
Datasets (MuJoCo, ground truth = exact joint state): drawer (prismatic), door (hinge), gripper (Panda hand, two sliding fingers), valve (ball-valve lever). Each: 300 episodes (150 dev-domain, 150 eval-domain; 10 scenes per domain; scenes are randomised lighting, floor/background texture, camera pose and field of view, distractors), 20 frames per episode (6000 frames), binary open/closed label by a fixed threshold with exclusion bands on both sides (drawer: closed <= 2 cm, open >= 6 cm; door: closed <= 10 deg, open >= 30 deg; gripper: closed <= 8 mm, open >= 25 mm per finger; valve: lever along the pipe = open, across = closed). Each episode has several open/close cycles in random order. Generator checks (all four): best time-only classifier, cross-validated by episode, has error .495 / .489 / .482 / .495 (drawer / door / gripper / valve; required >= .35); open share .504 / .500 / .503 / .498; object fully in image 1.000; 96 of 96 frames viewed by the builder agree with the label (24 per dataset).
Hypotheses and analysis are those of PREREGISTRATION.md sections 3-5, applied to the four SIM4 datasets: 'natural shift' means the dev-domain to eval-domain shift created by the randomised scenes plus 30 corruption cells on the eval pool. Splits by episode: dev-domain 100 train / 50 calibration; eval-domain 50 audit / 100 evaluation. C1 counts a dataset when Q beats R0 by >= .01 in its natural cell (second-largest per-dataset value for the bootstrap p); C3 counts a dataset when BA-full beats LA by >= .02 at m = 100 with episode-bootstrap lower bound > 0 (third-largest); C2 and C4 as registered; Holm over the four p-values.
Limits stated in advance: simulation only; one engine; the shift is designed by the authors, not natural; the magnitude of the shift was not calibrated against the frozen reader (forbidden before registration), and the darkest eval scenes may collapse the reader; builder-chosen counting rules for C1 and C3 are fixed here. A confirmatory claim here is a claim about these simulated datasets only.
