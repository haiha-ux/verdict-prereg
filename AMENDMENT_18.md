Corrections:
1. Retrospective k = 1 is the causal rule, so 36 of 216 cells are duplicates by construction; prediction (b) is trivial. In causal mode the first k - 1 gaps of an episode use M_k with 1/kk! (tc2_cert.py:44): conservative, not unsound.
2. Estimators: V1 is a fair estimate for k = 1 (2-4x the held-out need) but 20-530x too large for k >= 2 (it differentiates the noisy z and e is added on top). 'k = 1 is best' and (e) therefore reflect the Lebesgue amplification and the large e more than the M estimator (with held-out-minimal M, k = 1 still abstains less than k = 2 in 5 of 6 cells at m = 4, 8). Median-of-5 and SPRT average over 5 nodes (up to 2 s of lag): beating them says little.
3. 'Error approximately 0 against the reader' is valid but mostly empty: at q = 1.0 the certificate decides a median of only 5% of the between-node frames (about none at k = 3); fewer than 100 between-node decisions occur in 41 of 108 q = 1.0 cells; 51 of the 141 zero-error cells are error-free by construction; ties with linear/hold/EMA are all 0 = 0 and carry no information (at q = 1.0 strictly better than linear in 1/54 cells, than hold in 8/54, than EMA in 19/54, worse in none). At equal ERROR (zero reader error) an oracle-tuned flat hold band abstains less than the certificate in 5 of 9 (dataset, m) cells and more in 3 (mutex m = 4: .337 vs .649); a dev-calibrated hold band abstains 15-27 points less at m = 2 for 1-9 reader errors. The flip floor is .5-7% of frames, the certificate abstains 33-88%, dominated by the margin |z - tau| <= e. A better test is risk-coverage on between-node frames at equal error; not run.
4. Theorem 2: the logic is correct, but (i) section 3 of theory/94 still says 2 w_max / g and 'to be made precise' while section 7 gives 4 w_max / g (use section 7); (ii) extra assumptions are needed: |s - tau| > 2 w_max outside the crossing windows, node geometry and the Lebesgue sums for extrapolation (1 / 3 / 7 for k = 1, 2, 3), and M, e must be true bounds; (iii) it does NOT apply to the measured data: with the calibrated constants 2 w_max is 10-77 logits, larger than the whole z range (about 23-25 logits), and 99-100% of frames satisfy |z - tau| <= 2 w_max, so it explains none of the observed abstention. Theorem 3 is plausible, unverified; its e/g term needs a slope upper bound g_max and the M h^k term needs slack in the derivative budget.
5. Prediction (c): TRUE under the natural reading (191/216), but it holds automatically in the 141 cells where the certificate has zero error; under 'strictly better than every baseline' it is 61/216, i.e. FALSE. (a) false, (b) true-trivial, (d) false, (e) true with the caveat above.
