# Amendment 3 (append-only) — corrections to Amendment 2 after independent review, 2026-10-27
An independent critic (report kept in the authors' repository: .ai/runs/2026-10-27-critic-t2-behavior.md) reproduced result.json byte for byte and found the following; Amendment 2 stays as published and is qualified here.
1. BLOCKER for every BEHAVIOR-1K conclusion: in every episode the closed frames lie in t in [0, 41] s and the open frames in t >= 58 s, so the label is a function of time since the episode start; the phase-only baseline has error 0.0 in all 31 cells. The clean probe error (.003) cannot be attributed to the cabinet door (7 of the 48 visually checked frames did not show the cabinet and the probe is still right on 99.7% of the frames). Class balancing does not remove this and makes every pool exactly 50/50, so Q (quantile matching to a rate of about .5) knows the prior by design; its recovery (.957 pooled) is largely a design effect. Time-balancing is impossible in task-0004 (no common time range).
2. 'Run exactly as registered' in Amendment 2 is too strong: the group split, seed, C grid, the reading of C2 and the definition of LAx were chosen by the builder in Amendment 1, after the data and the confound were known. This is a self-registration, not a registry review.
3. Line 25 of experiments/out/t2/locks.txt (the SHA-256 of feat_behavior.npz) was appended after feature extraction under a 'pre-run' header; the published lock list has 24 lines. Only the Wayback snapshot of 06:27:00 UTC proves that no feature existed before 06:31:00 UTC.
4. Only 41 of the 48 visually checked frames could be judged (the registration text says '0 errors in 48 frames').
5. Hypotheses by the letter: C1 is falsified (one dataset; the single natural cell has no gap); C2 is not met and almost unattainable (the AUC<.9 group has 3 cells in 2 clusters; recovery >= .79 elsewhere); C3 descriptive only, its narrow interval rests on one fixed audit pool of 20 episodes; C4 fails marginally (R0A at m=200: .9395, interval [.900, .972]).
6. result.json contains NaN and -Infinity (not strict JSON).
Allowed statement (exploratory): on 30 synthetic corruption cells of one simulated scene, quantile matching recovered about .95 of the gap between the development threshold and an oracle threshold (mean error R0 .150, Q .058, oracle .054). Not allowed: that the reader recognises the door, any claim on natural shift, any claim about prevalence .047, any confident claim that auditing both candidates saves labels.
Next steps (not yet done): evaluation at natural prevalence with bootstrap over episodes; a dataset whose label is not monotone in time (another BEHAVIOR task, RBO with several cycles, a still-image dataset); masking or cropping the cabinet region with the simulator's segmentation.
