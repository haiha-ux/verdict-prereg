## (4) Theorem 2 / Theorem 3 (theory/94 sec 7)
- Proof logic VALID: abstain => |p-tau| <= w => |s-tau| <= |s-p|+|p-tau| <= 2w <= 2w_max; inside a monotone window |s-tau| >= g|t-c| => length <= 4w_max/g per crossing. Correct constant is 4, not the 2 w_max/g still written in sec 3 (line 18, with "(to be made precise)" kept) -> inconsistent text. "Nothing beyond MVT" is fine (Lagrange remainder is sec 2).
- Missing assumptions: (i) the outside-window hypothesis |s-tau| > 2w_max is an extra assumption, not derived (it is stated in parentheses); no tangential touches; finitely many, separated windows; (ii) w_max = sup over t of R_k(t) + e Lambda_k(t) depends on node selection and, for causal/extrapolation, on Lambda_k = 1,3,7 and c_k that are NOT "k only" in the sense of the equispaced interpolation bound; the C^k bound must hold on the hull of nodes and t (global M, which is the crippling uniformity); (iii) the first/last gaps (fewer nodes) need their own constants; (iv) e and M must be TRUE bounds, not dev estimates; (v) y = 1[s>tau] and the unobserved z_t versus s_t (above).
- Applicability to the data: FAILS. With the calibrated constants (k=1 causal, w_max = M_1 m/fps + e) 2 w_max = 21/27/10 (m=2), 35/44/14 (m=4), 61/77/24 (m=8) logits, larger than the whole 1-99 percentile range of z (about 23-25 logits); fraction of eval frames with |z-tau| <= 2 w_max = 1.000 (0.992 droid m2) (a7). So the hypothesis of Theorem 2 (abstention only near crossings) is false in every measured configuration and the theorem predicts nothing about the observed 33-88% abstention. UNVERIFIED as an explanation, VALID as a (trivial) theorem.
- Theorem 3 sketch plausible, UNVERIFIED. The e/g term is trivial (s1 and s1+e are both admissible and consistent, crossing shift e/s1'(c) <= e/g_min), so it needs slope upper bound g_max for a matching lower bound: optimality up to constants only if g_max/g_min is bounded. The M h^k term needs s1 with slack in the k-th-derivative budget so that s1 + (perfect spline vanishing at the nodes) stays in the class; and a worst-case instance/data, not every data. The rule that attains it up to constants includes trivial hold-with-margin; so "optimal up to constants" cannot distinguish the Lagrange enclosure from hold (matches the empirical ties). Not novel: optimal recovery / information-based complexity.
