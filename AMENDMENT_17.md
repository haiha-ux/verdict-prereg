# 95. Pre-registered test of the temporal smoothness certificate (theory/94) on dense robot video, design and lock, 2026-10-28
STATUS: written and locked BEFORE any dense frame was decoded and before any logit existed. theory/68 is NOT edited. Prior art (scout `.ai/runs/2026-10-28-scout-temporal-cert.md`): the pieces (Lagrange remainder, Lebesgue amplification, Landau-Kolmogorov, bounded-noise set-membership enclosures, robust satisfaction intervals for sampled STL, optimal recovery) are known; a combined certified-argmax rule and a continuous-time abstention bound were not found in ~20 queries (coverage partial). The test below asks whether the assumptions hold for real readers; it makes no novelty claim.
Data and reader (no tuning): LeRobot gripper-state video of mutex (20 fps), sirius (20 fps) and droid (15 fps), the dev_calib and eval episodes of the locked split (Amendment 12, experiments/out/lerobot/fit_<ds>-<ds>.json, 24/48 episodes for mutex; others as in the file); ALL native frames of those episodes (not the 1-2 Hz subsample). Reader = the locked DINOv2-small probe of the same dataset (within-dataset), logit z(t), threshold tau = the locked tS. Labels = measured gripper state (open = 1) with the locked band rule; band frames are decoded and used as nodes but are not scored.
Procedure (experiments/tc_dense.py, tc_cert.py): (1) calibrate on dev_calib episodes only: noise e_q = q-quantile of |z - SavGol(9,3)(z)|; derivative bound M_{k,q} = q-quantile of |k-th finite difference of the smoothed z| x fps^k, q in {.9, .99, 1.0}, k in {2,3,4}. (2) On eval episodes observe every m-th frame (m in {1,2,4,8,16}), enclose z between nodes by the Lagrange interpolant of k nodes with radius w = M/k! prod|t - t_j| + e sum|l_j|, decide 1 if p - w > tau, 0 if p + w < tau, otherwise abstain. (3) Score ALL native frames with label >= 0: error among non-abstained decisions (episode bootstrap 95% CI, 1000 draws) and the abstained fraction. (4) Baseline at equal abstention: linear interpolation of the nodes with a band b matched to the same abstained fraction.
Theorem 1 of theory/94 (soundness) predicts error 0 if the assumptions hold; the test is whether they do.
Predictions stated now (scored to the letter; any may fail): (a) with q = 1.0 and the dev-calibrated (e, M) the non-abstained error on eval episodes is > 0 in at least one dataset and one (k, m) with abstention < .8; (b) in no dataset does the abstained fraction fall below .3 at m >= 2 for q = 1.0; (c) k = 4 has an abstained fraction >= that of k = 3 in at least 2 of 3 datasets (m = 1, q = 1.0); (d) at equal abstention the interpolation baseline has an error <= the certificate's in at least 2 of 3 datasets for most (k, q, m) cells.
Not claimed: that the assumptions hold; that the certificate is useful; novelty; Tier-1 (no human labels; the sensor labels are not independent of the gripper kinematics that define smoothness). Cells are dependent (same episodes); no multiplicity correction; the CI is over episodes only.

## Addendum 17c (2026-10-28): corrections after the independent critic (.ai/runs/2026-10-28-critic-temporal.md). The reading in 17b ('conditions failed empirically', 'cannot separate smoothness from label mismatch') is WITHDRAWN.
Verified: the run is faithful to the lock (public commit e48a11b7 holds TC_CERT/TC_DENSE/TEMPORAL_CERT_94 with the same hashes; idx, z, result files are later), every n_err and n_decided reproduces, no leakage (dev_calib and eval episodes disjoint; the certificate never reads labels; calibrating on eval instead of dev changes e, M by 0-30%).
Corrections:
1. The smoothness assumption was NOT violated on held-out frames: at q = 1.0 the enclosure |z - p| <= w + e failed in 0 of 36 (dataset, k, m >= 2) cells; with the e slack removed, 4 of about 22,700 non-node frames fail (k = 2, m = 2). Every non-abstained error is a frame where the plain thresholded reader 1[z > tau] is already wrong (only 1 of 1097 errors over the 45 q = 1.0 cells has a decision different from 1[z > tau]). The reader's own error at the locked tau is .64% (mutex), .64% (sirius) and 11.7% (droid). Mutex and sirius errors lie within 2-5 frames of a label transition; droid errors have a median distance of 30 frames (a poor reader, not transitions). So the failures are reader-versus-sensor-label mismatch (about 100%) and not smoothness failure (about 0%); the test does separate them. 'Soundness fails' is only true with respect to the sensor label, not with respect to the reader's thresholded output. 'n_err > 0 in 15/15 cells' is trivial (mutex: one frame repeated); bootstrap CIs of the non-abstained error exclude 0 in 0/15 (mutex), 3/15 (sirius), 15/15 (droid) cells.
2. The calibrated bounds are loose: M is 11-300x larger than what held-out frames need (mutex k = 3, m = 8: 2.1e4 vs about 513) and inflates about 30x per order; the SavGol residual absorbs transition signal (top 1% of residuals are 37-44% within 10 frames of a transition). The high abstention at m >= 4 and the result 'higher order does not help' largely reflect this inflated M; 'higher order does not help' is therefore UNVERIFIED, and (c) is vacuous at m = 1 (identical abstention for every k).
3. Baseline matching defect: tc_cert.py line 110 bisects the band over all frames while line 79 counts only labelled frames, so the baseline abstains 0.3-3.5 points less in all 135 cells; after re-matching the baseline is better or equal in 44 / 41 / 42 of 45 cells (ties exist). At m = 1 both rules are identical. The EMA, SPRT, majority-vote and k = 1 (Lipschitz) baselines named in theory/94 section 6 were NOT run; a margin-only band at native rate and a band fixed on dev were not run either. 'No advantage' holds only for this three-dataset test, this e/M estimator and the interpolation baseline.
4. The droid sentence 'with q = .9 the abstention is 1-2%' is false for droid (.107-.120); in mutex and sirius it is .01-.03 for k = 2, m <= 2.
5. The interpolation is retrospective (a frame between nodes uses the next node): this is not an online monitor.
6. Prediction scoring: (a) TRUE to the letter but the mechanism is reader error; (b) FALSE (mutex .19, sirius .15 at m = 2), its interpretation is unverified; (c) TRUE but vacuous; (d) TRUE in direction, with the corrected count above.
Allowed wording: 'pre-registered test on three robot datasets; the smoothness assumptions calibrated on development episodes were not violated on held-out frames; non-abstained errors coincide with the thresholded reader's own errors; calibrated bounds are loose and cause high abstention; retrospective interpolation'. Not allowed: certified/sound decisions about the true state; 'assumptions fail'; 'no benefit' in general; any novelty, optimality or online-monitor claim; Theorems 2-3 remain unproved (UNVERIFIED).
What this does support: with the reader's thresholded output as the target, the enclosure rule between frames made essentially no error in these data; the value of the test is that the smoothness assumption was not the problem. It is not a result about the true state.
