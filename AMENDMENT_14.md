## Addendum 14b (before any feature was computed; CCT images fetched 2750/2750, 0 failures; Wellington and Serengeti fetch in progress)
Locked now: experiments/ct_fetch.py (already in locks), experiments/ct_run.py (stages feat, run). Clarifications to the plan, written before any feature/logit existed:
1. C4 audit size is counted in SITES: k in {5, 10, 20} audit sites (25 frames each) drawn from the union of audit sites of the 5 folds; the audited rule is R0 of each site's own fold; truth = pooled R0 error on the evaluated frames of all folds (pool P0). Betting bound on site-cluster error sums vs frame-level Clopper-Pearson on the same frames.
2. C3 is evaluated per case (fold x pool x rule in {R0, Q}); coverage = share of cases with realised regret <= rho; with 5 folds x 3 sets x up to 3 pools the case count is small and is reported as a count, not tested.
3. Pools PH/PL are formed inside each fold (sites of the fold with metadata empty share >= .60 / <= .20); a fold-pool needs >= 150 frames and >= 10 of each class, else skipped. C2 uses the fold-pools with |label prior - dev prior| >= .20.
4. Thresholds are computed once per fold-pool; the site bootstrap resamples sites but does not recompute thresholds (approximation, stated).
5. PX (cross-set) pools of the plan are NOT part of ct_run.py and will only be run as a secondary analysis after a further lock if time permits.
6. Probe recipe: t2_run.fit_probe unchanged (logistic, C by calibration log-loss, source error-minimising threshold); calibration = 25% of the training sites.

What these numbers do and do not show (critic-verified):
1. Constant-prediction baseline: in CCT and Wellington the probe is not better than predicting 'animal' always (Wellington R0 equals the majority rule in all 5 folds; CCT R0 is worse than majority in 2/5 folds). Regrets there say little about threshold choice. Only Serengeti S3 is an informative set.
2. C2 consists of 3 label-selected Serengeti S3 PH fold-pools (27 sites). CCT and Wellington have no pool with |prior shift| >= .20 (PL is about P0). The +.24 is close to arithmetic of Prop C' at AUC .98 (a known limitation); it is not a result across sets and says nothing about natural shift without label selection.
3. C3: 54/56 covered; both misses are quantile matching in Serengeti PH pools with prior shift .28 and .32; unshifted pools 50/50, shifted pools 4/6; median rho is 14x the realised regret, the 'informative' bar (width <= .30) is weak, cases are dependent. It is not evidence that the certificate is calibrated or validated.
4. C4: one fixed audit pool per set (35-50 sites); coverage 1.0 is over 2000 subsamples of that pool, not over independent site splits; the bound is 1.9-10.6x the pooled error. No coverage guarantee was tested. Frame-level Clopper-Pearson .71-.97 is subject to the same single-pool limit.
5. The prediction (b) of the plan (all rules within .03 of the oracle in unshifted pools) is FALSIFIED: Otsu is off by .32/.40 (CCT, Wellington) and quantile matching by .044 in Serengeti.
6. Serengeti missing frames are not random: all 56 failed downloads (HTTP 404, 1.5%) are animal-labelled frames (100% vs 46.7% overall), in 34 sites; the set is not complete and error rates are conditional on retrievable files.
