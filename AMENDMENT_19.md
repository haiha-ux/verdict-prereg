# 97. Risk-coverage test of the temporal certificate on between-node frames (design and lock, 2026-10-28, BEFORE the run)
STATUS: locked before `tc3_rc.py` was run. Motivation (critic of Amendment 18, .ai/runs/2026-10-28-critic-temporal2.md): the zero-error claim was empty because the certificate decided few between-node frames and was compared at equal abstention with tied zeros; the better test is risk-coverage on between-node frames with operating points set WITHOUT an oracle. No novelty claim; Theorem 3 not addressed here.
Data, reader, calibration: as theory/95-96 (locked z_<ds>.npz; mutex 48, sirius 50, droid 29 eval episodes; dev_calib episodes for every calibration). Only between-node labelled frames count (frame index not a multiple of m; m in {2, 4, 8}); causal (online) rules; every rule gives each frame a MARGIN (abstain below a threshold theta; sweep theta): cert_k1 and cert_k2 (margin = |p - tau| / w, w from the q = 1.0, V1 constants fitted on dev_calib; p = Lagrange value from the last k nodes), hold (|last node - tau|), EMA (alpha .5, |ema - tau|). Predicted state = side of the rule's centre.
Metrics: AURC = mean over coverage levels of the cumulative risk of the most confident frames (lower is better), against (i) the reader's own thresholded output 1[z_t > tau] and (ii) the sensor label; dev-calibrated operating points: theta* = smallest threshold with dev_calib risk <= eps, eps in {0, .002, .01}, then eval coverage and eval risk (reader target).
Predictions (scored to the letter): (a) cert_k1 has a lower AURC (reader target) than hold in >= 7 of the 9 (dataset, m) cells (the radius grows with the time since the last node, which hold ignores). (b) cert_k2 has a higher AURC than cert_k1 in >= 6 of 9 cells. (c) With eps = .002 the dev-calibrated cert_k1 has an eval reader-target risk <= .005 in >= 7 of 9 cells (calibration transfers). (d) At eps = .002 the eval coverage of cert_k1 exceeds that of hold by >= .02 in >= 5 of 9 cells.
Not claimed: certified decisions about the true state, novelty, optimality, Tier-1, A*. Cells are dependent (same episodes); frames within an episode are dependent; no CI is computed (descriptive); label risk reported only as a second view.

## Addendum 19b (2026-10-28): result of the single locked run (result_tc3.json SHA-256 e1d22bcedcb0c89e8e37b5e735185aaf2c75221b6c599e407f01df7717b66732; scoring script experiments/tc3_summary.py, output experiments/out/tc/summary3.txt). Independent critic pending.
Run once after the lock (Amendment 19 pushed 14:29:19Z). 9 (dataset, m) cells; between-node labelled frames: 4.4k-21k per cell.
Predictions scored to the letter: (a) FALSE: cert_k1 has a lower reader-target AURC than hold in only 4 of 9 cells (threshold 7). In the m = 2 cells the two rules coincide or tie (a gap of one frame barely changes the radius); at m = 4 the picture is mixed (mutex .0006 vs .0005, sirius .0005 vs .0004, droid .0052 vs .0056); at m = 8 cert_k1 is lower in all three datasets (mutex .0022 vs .0027, sirius .0027 vs .0035, droid .0125 vs .0145). (b) TRUE: cert_k2 has a higher AURC than cert_k1 in 7 of 9 cells. (c) TRUE: with eps = .002 the dev-calibrated cert_k1 has an eval reader-target risk <= .005 in 8 of 9 cells (calibration transfers; the exception is droid m = 8, .0055). (d) FALSE: at eps = .002 the eval coverage of cert_k1 exceeds that of hold by >= .02 in 3 of 9 cells (mutex m = 8 .785 vs .644, sirius m = 8 .690 vs .429, droid m = 8 .485 vs .312); at m = 2, 4 it does not.
Reading: the age-normalised margin of the certificate (|last node - tau| divided by a radius that grows with the time since the node) gives a better risk-coverage trade-off than the plain hold margin only when the observation gap is long (m = 8, 0.4-0.5 s); at short gaps they coincide; a second-order extrapolation (k = 2) is worse. With dev-calibrated operating points (no oracle) the eval risk stays near the target, so the margin ordering transfers within a dataset. The gain at m = 8 is a gain over the hold margin, not over every baseline; the SPRT and median-of-5 baselines were not run in this test; the label-risk view (AURC against the sensor label) mirrors the reader-risk view except for droid, where the label risk is dominated by the reader's own error (.027-.049). The margin is essentially the Lipschitz interpretation of 'how long ago did I last see the frame'; this is not claimed as new.
Not claimed: certified decisions about the true state, novelty, optimality, Tier-1, A*. Cells are dependent; no CI; 3 robot gripper datasets with within-dataset readers; Theorem 3 not addressed.
