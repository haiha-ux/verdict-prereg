# Amendment 16 (append-only) — PX cross-set pools: design and lock, 2026-10-28, BEFORE the run
What: the cross-set pools (PX) that the plan (theory/90) locked but did not run. For each ordered pair of the three verified sets (A -> B, A != B; 6 pairs) a probe is trained on all sites of A (75/25 train/calibration by site, 10 calibration seeds) and evaluated on all frames of B (all its sites). Natural shift: different regions, cameras, species and, importantly, different positive (animal) prior: CCT .86, Wellington .82, Serengeti S3 .47 in the sampled frames. Code: experiments/ct_px.py (uses ct_run functions unchanged). WCS is NOT included (identity with iWildCam expert labels unconfirmed).
Reported per pair (mean over the 10 seeds and range): AUC, calibration prior vs target prior, error of R0/Q/Otsu/oracle and regret, error of the constant 'animal' rule, identified-set certificate (band +-.30 around the calibration prior) for R0 and Q and whether it covered the realised regret. No test, no Holm; 6 pairs, dependent (shared sets); frames of one site are dependent (no site bootstrap is done here, so no CI).
Predictions stated before the run (any may fail): (a) Q is worse than R0 where the priors differ by >= .30 (pairs involving Serengeti) and close to R0 for CCT <-> Wellington; (b) the certificate covers R0 and Q where |prior shift| <= .30, and may fail beyond; (c) AUC drops relative to the within-set cross-fits (.85 / .73 / .98 for CCT / Wellington / Serengeti), in at least 4 of 6 pairs; (d) the constant rule is competitive with R0 for pairs whose target is CCT or Wellington.
Tier-1 is not affected (3 sets, WCS unconfirmed). Nothing here is claimed as validation.

## Addendum 16c (2026-10-28): corrections after the independent critic (.ai/runs/2026-10-28-critic-camtrap-px.md)
Verified: the run is faithful to the lock (lock commit 58bfba4 13:13:52Z holds ct_px.py, ct_run.py, the design; result written after); re-running all 60 cells (6 pairs x 10 seeds) reproduces result_px.json exactly; every number in the 16b table is correct; no target data enters the fit; R0/Q/Otsu are label-free, the oracle uses target labels in-sample.
Corrections to 16b:
1. Q vs R0 is NOT stable across seeds: Q beats R0 in 9/10 seeds (CCT->Ser), 1/10 (Wel->Ser), 5/10 (Ser->CCT, SD .042: a coin flip), 9/10 (Ser->Wel). 'Q better than R0 in 3 of 4 pairs' holds on means only. Q imposes the source predicted-positive rate (.94, .98, .46, .46) on targets with prior .46 or .82-.86, so its error cannot fall below |rho - target prior|; the comparison is between two prior-blind rules and does not test whether Q works. 'R0 badly off, Q partly corrects it' holds only for Ser->Wel (R0 positive rate .295, Q .461); elsewhere it is speculation and is withdrawn.
2. In the four pairs with a CCT or Wellington target the oracle is 'always animal' (positive rate .98-1.0): there 'regret' is excess over the constant rule. On Serengeti targets the right constant baseline is 'always empty' (.459), and R0 (.529, .518) is worse than that.
3. Certificate: the Ser->CCT row (R0 4/10, Q 2/10) must be read with the others; in the four shifted pairs the target prior lies outside the +-.30 band in 9-10 of 10 seeds, so those failures are the band assumption being false, not a test of the certificate; the two within-band pairs are trivial (oracle = constant rule, regret <= .03). 'Vacuous' for the width-.43 case is WRONG: the certificate (.432) tracks the realised regret (.402; per-seed Spearman .90; R0 covered 9/10 with margins down to .002); the right statement is that its width exceeds the plan's .30 informativeness bar.
4. Predictions: (a) FALSE on means with an unstable sign; its second half ('Q close to R0 for CCT<->Wellington') is true and trivial and was not scored; (b) true but trivial; (c) 6/6 against the target set's within-set AUC but 4/6 against the source set's; CCT->Wel .655 lies inside the within-set fold range (.643-.770); (d) true, with the baseline correction of item 2.
5. The sentence 'Otsu is a prevalence-dependent heuristic' is unverified (prevalence and set identity are confounded). The seeds vary only A's train/calibration split: no confidence interval over target sites. The 3 sets are shared across pairs, effective sample 3; the four shifted pairs are one contrast, Serengeti vs the others.
Allowed: 'under between-set shift with prior differences of .35-.40 the source threshold and quantile matching both had regret of .16-.40 over the oracle, and neither was consistently better'; 'the +-.30-band certificate tracked the realised regret in the Serengeti-source pairs it covered and failed where the target prior lay outside the band'.
