## 1. Implementation (tc_cert.py)
- Enclosure correct: remainder `M/k! prod|t-tj|` and `e*sum|l_j|` (:57) are the textbook bound; valid also at episode ends where t lies outside the node hull (lo clamped :53), still valid since the remainder formula holds for extrapolation. Node choice (centered window) affects width only, not soundness. Offline/non-causal: decision at a frame between nodes uses the FUTURE node; this is retrospective interpolation, not an online monitor (wording issue, section 5).
- At m=1 every frame is a node: p=z_t, remainder 0, Lambda=1, so the certificate is literally `|z-tau| > e` for all k (identical abstention and error for k=2,3,4 in every dataset, confirmed). No temporal content in the m=1 cells (9 of 45 per dataset).
- e estimate (SavGol residual) conflates noise with real fast signal: of the top-1% |z-SG(z)| frames in dev_calib, 44% (mutex) / 37% / 39% lie within 10 frames of a label transition vs 20% / 17% / 24% of all frames (a5). So e_{q=1} = 4.2/5.1/2.5 logits is partly the transition edge that SG(9,3) cannot follow, assigned to "noise" instead of to M. Also smoother residuals underestimate temporally correlated noise. e and M are therefore not separately identified by this protocol.
- M estimate: |k-th diff of SG output| x fps^k is NOT a bias-low estimate here; it is far too HIGH relative to what held-out frames need (a5/a6/a7): smallest M that covers z (with the e slack) on eval frames: mutex k=3,m=8: 513 vs M_calib 2.1e4 (41x); k=4: 2.5e3 vs 7.5e5 (300x); sirius/droid need ~0 (e alone covers) at m>=4. M_k grows ~30x per order (258 -> 5.8e3 -> 1.5e5 droid) because it measures smoothed-noise differences, not sup|s^(k)|. Consequence: abstention at m>=4 (.77 at m=4, .89 at m=8) is mostly an artifact of M-looseness: with the oracle M (eval-fitted, illustration only, NOT a result) mutex k=3 abstention is .18 (m=4), .37 (m=8) instead of .77/.89, and error unchanged (~1e-4). So (b) (abstention vs h) and (c) (k=4 vs k=3) say more about the M estimator than about the signal.
- Direct test of the enclosure assumptions (S_k)+(N_e) on eval, observed z vs interval: at q=1.0, non-node frames with |z_t - p| > w + e: 0 in every one of the 36 (dataset,k,m>=2) cells at q=1.0 (k in {2,3,4}, m in {2,4,8,16}) (a3); with no extra slack |z_t - p| > w: 4 frames of ~22,700 at k=2,m=2 (max ratio 1.21), 0 elsewhere (a6). With the remainder alone (e=0) it is violated thousands of times at m=2 - the noise term is necessary and near tight at m=2. So at q=1.0 the interpolation-enclosure assumptions HELD on held-out episodes (with a loose M). At q=.9 coverage fails on 0.1-2% of non-node frames at m=2-4 as expected by construction.

## 4. Baseline fairness
- Linear-interpolation band: the k=2 certificate has the same centre (linear interpolant), differs only in band shape (e + parabola vs flat b), so k=2 vs baseline compares band shapes; k=3,4 mix centre and band. Informative only for "does the remainder-shaped band beat a flat band at equal abstention": answer here no, because both are filtering low-margin frames and the residual errors are confident reader errors; the baseline gets lower error mainly because to reach the same abstention it needs b > e at the nodes, i.e. drops marginal-confidence node frames - that is a logit-margin effect, not a temporal one.
- Matching is eval-fitted (bisection on the eval abstention per cell), label-free (baseline never reads labels; labels only define the abstention denominator) but not deployable; present as risk-coverage curves instead.
- A reviewer would demand: (i) the k=1 (Lipschitz/step, RoSI-style constant-interpolation) sound baseline (not run, KS=2,3,4); (ii) causal filters at equal abstention: EMA with band, median/majority window, hysteresis (Schmitt), SPRT/CUSUM on the logit; (iii) a margin-only baseline `|z-tau|>b` at native rate (the m=1 rule; it equals the certificate there); (iv) calibrated-confidence selective classification (conformal/risk-coverage) - since errors are reader errors, a better-calibrated reader or a margin rule is the competitor; (v) a held-out operating point (b fixed on dev) rather than eval bisection; (vi) labels from the reader itself (1[z>tau] on native frames) as the target to isolate temporal effects.
- "No advantage" is justified only as: in these 3 datasets, with this e/M estimator, at equal abstention a flat band on a linear interpolant has lower or equal error in ~91-98% of cells (lt+eq 44/41/42 of 45 after re-matching; 58-67% strict in the original json). Not justified as a general statement about the certificate.
