This is informed by the result of test 4 (theory/98 addendum 20b: the additive-distance margin of cone_all lost to the age-discounted hold); the diagnosis (the correct sweep for the lambda-scaled cone intersection is the maximum ratio |z_i - tau| / (e + L|t - t_i|) over nodes) was derived from the structure of Theorem 3 and then written here; it is a second stage, not a fresh pre-registration. No novelty claim (set-membership estimation; age-discounted confidence).
Data, calibration and metrics: as theory/98 (z_<ds>.npz, dev_calib for e, L = M_1 at q = 1.0 V1, and the age-discount parameter c*; between-node labelled frames, m in {2, 4, 8}, causal rules, 9 cells; AURC against the reader's own output and the sensor label; dev-calibrated operating points at eps in {0, .002, .01}).
Methods: cone_max (margin = max over all past nodes of the ratio; decision = side of the maximising node), cone_max_w5 (last 5 nodes), cert_k1 (last-node ratio), hold_c (|z_last - tau| / (age + c*)), hold.
Predictions (scored to the letter): (a) cone_max has a reader-target AURC <= cert_k1 in >= 7 of 9 cells (cells in which all methods coincide count as ties, not wins; wins are required in >= 4 cells with m in {4, 8}). (d) cone_max_w5 and cone_max have AURC within 5% (relative) in >= 8 of 9 cells.
Not claimed: certified decisions about the true state; novelty; any benefit against the sensor label; Tier-1; A*. Same dependence caveats as before; three robot gripper datasets.

Corrections:
1. Test 4 could not test the cone intersection: at the calibrated L (q = 1.0, the maximum slope) no older node binds at m = 8 and at most a few hundred frames bind at m = 2, 4; every binding node is among the last 5, hence cone_all = cone_w5 exactly; the intersection is never empty. With L scaled to 0.25 older nodes bind on 5-44% of frames and the intersection is still empty on <= 0.8%. So the 20b statement that the nearest cones dominate is an artefact of the large calibrated L, and test 4 had no power to show a benefit of intersecting.
2. The 20b diagnosis (additive vs scaled margin) is WRONG: an additive margin d - b age with b tuned on dev matches the ratio margin (m = 8 eval AURC 2.09 / 2.59 / 13.33 x1e-3 vs 2.18 / 2.67 / 12.52 for the ratio). The failing cause is the fixed slope L / fps, which is 3-13x steeper than the dev-optimal b. The 21b reading (an older node on the other side of tau dominates) is true but incomplete: on 1-6% of frames cone_max's state differs from the last node's and is wrong there 69-94% of the time; in more than 99% of those frames the last node is within the noise band, i.e. the rule discards the newest evidence at transitions; all such frames have ratio < 1 (in the certified region the two agree). Margin and state both contribute; 'the reader is noisy around tau' is untested.
3. 21b (c) is misreported: the coverage gain >= .02 holds in 1 of 9 cells (sirius m = 8, +.027), not none; the verdict (FALSE, needed >= 3) stands.
4. Tests 4 and 5 are not independent of each other or of tests 1-3 (same eval episodes, same calibration; test 5 designed after seeing test 4). The 'net outcome' sentence of 21b is withdrawn as written: 'set-membership refinements do not improve risk-coverage' is UNVERIFIED (test 4 was inactive at q = 1.0 L; test 5 changed state and margin together).
