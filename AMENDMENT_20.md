Diagnosis (stated after seeing the result, not a pre-registered claim): the margin chosen for cone_all, the ADDITIVE distance of tau outside I(t), ranks frames differently from the confidence ordering that the scaled-radius family implies. If the radius is scaled by lambda (abstain iff tau lies in the intersection of the cones with radii lambda (e + L age_i)), tau is excluded exactly when lambda < max_i |z_i - tau| / (e + L |t - t_i|); the correct sweep margin of the cone intersection is this maximum ratio, and the decision is the side of the maximising node. The additive margin |z_last - tau| - (e + L age) penalises age linearly in logit units, whereas the ratio penalises it multiplicatively, which is what the age-discounted hold favoured. So the pre-registered cone_all was the wrong sweep for a risk-coverage curve; the failed predictions (a), (b), (e) refute that margin, not Theorem 3 (an exact statement about the attainable set).
A follow-up with the maximum-ratio margin is registered separately (theory/99) before it is run; it is an exploratory second stage informed by this result.
