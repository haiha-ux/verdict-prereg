# Amendment 6 (append-only) — corrections to Amendment 5 after independent review of SIM4, 2026-10-27
An independent critic (report in the authors' repository: .ai/runs/2026-10-27-critic-sim4.md) reproduced the eval stage (claims identical byte for byte) and the Amendment 4 files match their registered hashes. Findings that qualify Amendment 5:
1. C1 is weaker than 'SUPPORTED' suggests. The registered rule used an episode bootstrap (p = .0025, Holm .010). The independent unit is the scene: the 10 eval scenes are shared by all four datasets and by the audit and evaluation pools. A bootstrap over scenes gives intervals containing 0 in 3 of 4 datasets and p of about .075 before Holm.
2. The advantage of quantile matching over the development threshold depends on an unchanged class prior (about 50%, enforced by the generator). With a prior shift (.35/.65) Q is worse than R0. The valve set is nearly separable (AUC 1.000, Otsu .005) and Otsu is at least as good as Q in 2 of 4 datasets.
3. C3: the point estimates lie outside their intervals because the bootstrap resamples audit frames with duplicates (which makes LA optimistic), not because of 40 versus 200 draws as Amendment 5 stated. A variant without duplicates gives P(difference >= .02) between .985 and .999, so 'not supported' is sensitive to this defect. Amendment 5 also reversed the sign wording: the BA-full bound is LOWER than the LA bound.
4. C4: the 20 of 80 coverage violations are not a split accident. Re-splitting the pools 300 times shows coverage falling with m (.93-1.0 at m = 50; .80-.91 at m = 200): the between-episode and between-scene variance is ignored by a frame-level Clopper-Pearson bound. An episode-level bound is the proposed fix and is not yet tested.
5. Provenance: experiments/out/sim4/locks_t2_s4.txt has a modification time of 15:24:56 (+07) and contains feature hashes under a 'pre-run' header; the start time of the feat stage was not logged. Only the Wayback snapshot of Amendment 4 proves no feature existed earlier.

Not allowed: a strong C1, any statement on natural shift or prior shift, that Q beats Otsu, or that 'not supported' for C3 shows that auditing two candidates does not save labels.
