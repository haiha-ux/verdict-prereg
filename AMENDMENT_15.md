# Amendment 15 (append-only) — 20 independent site-split replicates (design and lock, 2026-10-28, BEFORE any replicate was run)
Reason: the independent critic of Amendment 14d found that C3/C4 rest on one fold assignment and one audit pool per set. This amendment adds a robustness run with R = 20 independent re-draws of the site-to-fold and audit/eval assignment (seed [20261028, 77, r, set]) over the SAME sampled frames and features (no new image, no new feature). Code: experiments/ct_rep.py (calls the locked ct_run functions unchanged; hash in locks.txt).
Per replicate and set: C1 P0 regrets of R0/Q/Otsu, error of R0 and oracle, constant 'animal' error 1 - mean(label), AUC; C2 mean(Q - R0) in fold-pools PH/PL with |prior shift| >= .20 (with the number of such pools and which sets); C3 covered / n over cases (fold x pool x {R0, Q}) overall and in shifted pools; C4 betting-bound and frame-level Clopper-Pearson coverage of the pooled R0 error for k in {5, 10, 20} audit sites (2000 subsamples of the replicate's audit pool).
Pre-stated reading: C4 coverage is judged ACROSS replicates (mean over the 20 replicates; share of replicates with coverage < .95), not within one pool. A claim 'coverage >= .95' is allowed only if >= 19 of 20 replicates meet it for that set and k. C3 is reported as counts with the shifted/unshifted split; no pass/fail threshold. C2 is reported as the distribution over replicates, with the number of replicates in which any CCT or Wellington pool is shifted. No Holm. No claim that Q or the certificate 'works'. The set of replicates is not extended after seeing results; a replicate that crashes is reported.
Expected (stated before the run): CP undercovers at k = 5 and 10 in at least one set; betting stays >= .95; C2 positive only in Serengeti.
Tier-1 is not affected by this run (3 datasets, WCS unconfirmed, PX not run).
