# Amendment 7 (append-only) — E5: episode-level audit bounds on the stored SIM4 logits, 2026-10-27
E5 is a follow-up designed AFTER the C4 failure of SIM4 was seen (Amendments 5 and 6); it is therefore exploratory by construction, not confirmatory. It reuses the stored SIM4 features and probes (no new rendering, no new feature extraction) and is registered here BEFORE it is run (no experiments/out/e5/result.json exists at this moment).
Question: does a bound built on episodes, not frames, restore the coverage that the frame-level Clopper-Pearson bound loses (C4)?
Design (theory/75_e5_design.md): scored cells = the clean natural cell of each of the 4 datasets x 5 strategies (QA, R0A, LA, LAx, BA-full) x m in {20, 50, 100, 200} = 80 cells; audit sampling by clusters: e = m/5 episodes drawn without replacement from the 50 audit episodes, 5 random frames per episode, 200 draws; truth = error of the chosen threshold on the fixed 100-episode evaluation pool. One-sided 95% bounds (alpha/2 for BA-full and LAx): F = Clopper-Pearson on all 5e frames; T = r_bar + t_{e-1} s / sqrt(e) on per-episode error means; H = r_bar + sqrt(ln(1/alpha) / (2e)); CPe = Clopper-Pearson with n = e and k = floor(e r_bar + .5). Coverage = fraction of draws with truth <= bound, Wilson 95% intervals.
Predictions (fixed before running): P1 coverage of F is below .95 in at least 30% of the cells; P2 coverage of T is at least .95 in at least 85% of the cells; P3 coverage of H is at least .95 in at least 98% of the cells; P4 mean width of T is at most 1.6 times that of F and H is at least 1.5 times wider than T; P5 coverage of T does not fall monotonically with m (Spearman between m and mean coverage >= -.3).
Choices made by the builder where the design was silent (fixed here): the clean cell only is scored (not the average over 31 conditions), so the earlier 25% of C4 is not directly comparable; P5 is a Spearman over four points (rough); zero observed errors give T = 0 and T may fail at small m (no correction added); truth uses one fixed evaluation pool and audit and evaluation pools share the 10 scenes, so scene-level clusters are not handled.
