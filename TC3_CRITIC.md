## (5) Interpretation
- cert_k1 margin = |z_last - tau| / (age + c) (exact). This is time-decayed / staleness-aware (age-of-information) confidence in hold, the first-order Lipschitz safe-time rule (d > L*age + noise) used in event/self-triggered sampled-data control, i.e. a known heuristic with one parameter; the certificate contributes only the fixed value of c via e*fps/M1 (dev-estimated, 2-4x loose per critic-temporal2) and the ratio form (the sound decision is the difference d > M*age/fps + e, ranked here by ratio).
