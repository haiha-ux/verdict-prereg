Corrections from the critic (reproduced all 31 cells of two datasets to 1e-9; no leak; Holm correct):
1. C3 is an artefact of the label split. LA spends m/2 labels on choosing the threshold and audits with m/2; BA audits both candidates on all m at alpha/2. With zero errors the Clopper-Pearson gap alone is .0219 (> .02). On i.i.d. synthetic data without any shift the same contrast is .045-.049 at every error level from .025 to .32, and the SIM5 values (.032-.045) lie in that range and do not correlate with cell error (r = -.16). The claim 'union-bound audit saves labels' is therefore a statement about the cost of halving the sample, not about SIM5; SIM4 gave the same effect size (the different p-values come from third-largest-of-4 versus third-largest-of-5 and bootstrap skew). C3 must not be compared across SIM4 and SIM5 and is not a replication.
2. C1 is a floor effect. The most that Q can gain in the clean eval cell (err(R0) - err(oracle)) is .012 (microwave), .0025 (cabinet2), .0075 (boxlid), .005 (laptop), .117 (slider): only 2 of 5 datasets could reach +.01 while the rule needs 3, so C1 could not be met whatever Q did. Registering a claim whose threshold could not be reached was a design error of the authors' (the shift was not calibrated). Where there was room, slider improved by .105 (interval [.057, .148]) and microwave changed by -.005. Under the 30 corruption cells Q lowered mean error in all 5 datasets (e.g. slider .335 to .197). A pooled post hoc count over SIM4 and SIM5: among the 6 datasets with room, Q beat R0 by >= .01 in 4 (door, gripper, valve, slider), not in drawer (+.008) and microwave (-.005); three datasets had no room.
3. Prevalence confound not stated in Amendment 10: every scene and domain has exactly 50% open frames, so Q's target rate (the dev rate) equals the eval rate by construction (the same condition as Theorem A). SIM5 says nothing about prior shift, where Proposition C' shows quantile matching can fail.
4. C3 per-dataset intervals do not contain their point estimates in boxlid, laptop and slider: audit-scene resampling with replacement duplicates frames between the two halves of LA, which lowers the LA bound (slider .326 to .311). The intervals are biased by about .005-.015 and are not 95% intervals of the point; they are not to be used. Amendment 10's claim that this defect was fixed was wrong: only the draw count was equalised.
5. C4: the audit pool is exactly 200 frames, so m = 200 is the whole pool and 'valid' is 0 or 1 for QA, R0A and BA; the 31 cells of one dataset share one audit pool (one realisation, not 31); frames are not independent (4 per scene). The C4 failure count is a diagnostic, not a coverage rate.
Allowed reading: on five author-built MuJoCo families under a designed shift, C1, C2 and C4 were not met and C3 was met only as an artefact of label splitting; C1 failed because of a floor effect, not because Q fails; under corruption Q lowered mean error in all five families when the open share is known (50%). This is a hash-locked, author-run replicate, not independent, not natural data, not five independent datasets.
Not claimed: that Q does or does not help in general; that auditing two candidates saves labels under shift; anything about prior shift or real data.
